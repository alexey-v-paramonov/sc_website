Streaming.Center version 3.33.0 has been released: fade in/out points, remote backups, and more.

- Custom fade points: choose where a track finishes fading in and where the next track starts.
- Improved waveform editor: draggable markers, precise time controls, mouse-wheel zoom, and visible fade curves.
- Fractional durations: crossfades support values such as 0.5 and 1.25 seconds.
- Added a DJ buffer setting to hold incoming audio and absorb brief network interruptions. Larger buffers add delay; zero minimizes delay but increases the risk of interruptions.
- Added a separate DJ crossfade duration to adjust transition length independently for tracks and DJs.
- Smoother DJ transitions: both sides fade regardless of the track crossfade mode, including during rapid connections and disconnections.
- Added remote backups to the admin panel, with support for S3 storage, Dropbox, Google Drive, FTP, and more.
- Admins can now add notifications for broadcaster accounts.
